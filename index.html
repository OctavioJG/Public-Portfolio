About Me

Connected through
design and delivery.

I’m a Toronto-based design and operations professional with more than 15 years of experience across architectural projects, design research, and global creative services. My background combines a Bachelor of Architecture from Mexico with a Master of Design in Strategic Foresight and Innovation from OCAD University. Together, these foundations shape how I approach projects: understanding how people use spaces and services, seeing the wider system, and connecting design decisions with practical delivery.

My career began in residential architecture, commercial interiors, and retail environments, with responsibilities spanning design development, technical documentation, and project coordination. I expanded that experience into workplace research before joining a global healthcare software company, where my work evolved from design research into creative and digital leadership. Across these roles, research remained a foundation for understanding needs, testing assumptions, and translating findings into recommendations.

In my recent work, I led multidisciplinary teams across multiple regions and developed the processes and frameworks that supported their delivery. This included creative service planning, workflow improvement, brand and website integration, and the coordination of conferences and office environments. Working across physical and digital settings has strengthened my ability to connect concepts, people, resources, and implementation.

That same interest in how organizations work informs my growing focus on AI readiness and responsible adoption. Through use-case research, governance planning, and continued study in AI strategy and project management, I explore how emerging tools can support useful outcomes with appropriate human judgment and oversight. Across my work, I’m motivated by understanding a challenge, developing a clear response, and working with others to carry it through.
