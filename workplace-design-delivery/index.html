Workplace Design & Delivery

Developing and delivering a flexible workplace for everyday office work, training, and group gatherings.

The brief

An innovation consultancy needed a workplace that could support its staff and host visitors for training, focus groups, and facilitated sessions. The program combined open work areas, private offices, meeting rooms, a reference library, and shared amenities. It also needed space for presentations and town halls within the everyday working environment.

Understanding the space

The project occupied a vacant ground-floor commercial unit in a new building. Its ceiling height made a mezzanine possible, subject to the required approvals. Working with the client and principal architect, I studied how staff workspaces, visitor activities, shared facilities, and circulation could fit across two levels.

I developed schematic drawings and presentation materials to compare options and incorporated feedback through design approval.

Designing for adaptability

The stair around the elevator connected the two levels and helped organize the spaces around it. Stepped seating along the stair gave staff a place for informal gatherings and town halls.

In the open work areas, movable units associated with the reference library doubled as standing workstations. Timber volumes with glazed partitions housed private offices and meeting rooms, with a shared leisure area between them.

On the mezzanine, movable partitions allowed one large meeting space to serve as an auditorium or divide into two rooms for training and focus groups. The open kitchen supported staff lunches and hosted gatherings.

Developing the technical package

After design approval, I produced plans, elevations, sections, furniture layouts, finish drawings, and details for the modular millwork. I coordinated the architectural package with structural, electrical, plumbing, and safety specialists. The drawings recorded lighting, power, plumbing, and HVAC requirements alongside the information contractors needed to price, fabricate, and install bespoke components.

I also prepared and submitted the permit and licence documentation, incorporating the relevant specialist inputs.

From documentation to handover

The practice delivered construction through its internal construction team. I issued drawings, worked directly with contractors, and supervised work on site against the design documentation. I maintained budgets, updated Gantt schedules, and prepared progress reports on cost, timing, and delivery through completion and handover.

The completed workplace brought daily office functions, training, and informal gatherings into one space. The mezzanine, stair seating, and reconfigurable meeting area gave the client several ways to use the same footprint.
