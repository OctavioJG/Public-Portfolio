Workplace Planning

Using workplace research and functional analysis to inform space planning and future investment decisions.

← Architecture & Built Environment← Research & StrategyCase study

Project Information

Role
Workplace Consulting Intern / Analyst
Project type
Workplace research, functional programming, and portfolio planning
Deliverables
Functional and technical program, space-planning scenarios, analytical diagrams, reports, and client presentations

The brief

The client needed a clearer understanding of how its existing workplaces supported day-to-day operations before making decisions about future space and investment. The consulting team examined how teams used space, which functions needed dedicated settings, and where more flexible arrangements could accommodate changing needs.

Understanding how people worked

The team combined stakeholder interviews, focus groups, workplace observations, surveys, and functional analysis. Together, these methods helped the team understand how people worked, which groups needed to work near one another, and where existing layouts supported or constrained their activities.

I supported interviews and focus groups, documented discussions, and organized findings as the study progressed. I then synthesized interview transcripts and workshop notes into themes the team could use to assess workplace requirements.

Translating research into planning scenarios

I prepared analytical diagrams and developed floor-plan scenarios in AutoCAD. The diagrams made relationships between teams and activities visible. The scenarios helped the team explore how alternative layouts could accommodate those relationships and where dedicated or shared space might be appropriate.

I also assembled preliminary and final reports and prepared presentation materials for client reviews. These materials connected the research findings to the planning options under discussion.

Developing the functional and technical program

I contributed to documenting and presenting the functional and technical program. It brought together operational requirements, employee needs, and considerations for future growth and adaptability. The program gave the client a basis for evaluating spatial requirements and considering future investment priorities.

My contribution spanned research support, synthesis, spatial scenarios, and reporting. The work connected what people and teams needed from their workplace to decisions about how space could support them.
