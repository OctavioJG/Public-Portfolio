Interactive Digital Experience

Coordinating the design and delivery of an interactive conference installation after late content revisions compressed production to four business days.

← Creative & Design OperationsCase study

The brief

For a healthcare industry conference, we set out to create an interactive installation that showed how a single incident could unfold across people, processes, and technology. The experience needed to bring several perspectives into one story, help attendees recognize operational pain points, and show where software tools could support the people responding to the incident.

We structured the experience around the incident as it unfolded. That gave attendees a clear sequence to follow as they moved between perspectives. Handoffs, delays, and gaps in visibility provided specific moments to show where a tool or framework could help.

My role

I led experience design and coordinated delivery with the internal content team and an external agency. I translated the narrative into interactions, developed wireframes and mockups, and provided creative direction as the agency developed the installation. I also managed the design timeline, oversaw final quality checks, and led the on-site display setup.

Four days to deliver

Late narrative revisions effectively reduced a three-week production window to four business days. The event date was fixed, and the agency needed to know how the revised story would affect the layout, motion, and interactions before it could complete production.

I focused the remaining work on three priorities: keeping the story clear, making the interactions coherent, and maintaining visual consistency across screens. I turned the revised narrative into rapid mockups that specified layout, motion, and visual direction. The mockups gave the agency a concrete production reference and gave the content team a shared view of how its edits would appear in the experience.

I kept feedback focused on the decisions the agency needed to move forward and set clear expectations about what we could deliver in the time available. That reduced the need for interpretation and limited rework during the remaining production days.

The result

The installation was fully functional and ready the night before the conference opened. The finished experience brought multiple perspectives into one incident narrative, showing where handoffs and information gaps affected the response and where software could help.

I oversaw the final quality checks and led the configuration of the on-site displays to ensure the experience was presented consistently across screens.

Project Credits
