Project Delivery

Connecting people, priorities, and resources to carry projects from planning through delivery.

A structured approach,
adapted to each project.

My delivery experience spans digital platforms, conferences, workplace environments, residential repairs, and technology procurement. I clarify scope and responsibilities, plan around milestones and dependencies, and coordinate the people and resources needed to move the work forward. Through budget tracking, quality reviews, and regular reporting, I help teams address issues and make informed decisions. I adapt the approach to each project’s scale and constraints, combining structured planning with iterative reviews and hands-on coordination.

Website Consolidation · 2025

Website Consolidation & Governance

Project at a Glance

A global software company consolidated five regional websites onto a shared platform. I led the six-month design workstream, managing scope, priorities, and progress reporting for a four-person design team. I worked with the Head of Digital, who reported to me and led the build with the web development team and an external agency.

Challenge

The corporate identity provided a starting point for the website. The design team needed to translate it into accessible colour and imagery treatments, responsive components, and reusable page templates. That work had to align with executive review dates, development dependencies, and the rollout sequence for five regional sites.

Delivery Approach

Planned around milestones and dependencies. I estimated the design effort, mapped it against executive reviews and development needs, and used a RACI matrix to clarify ownership. I prioritized the design system and launch-critical assets, then sequenced the work into design sprints.

Coordinated prototyping and approvals. I led accessibility testing and coordinated approval of the web design system, then translated marketing requirements into design briefs. Over three weeks, the team completed six prototyping cycles covering core pages and navigation. I packaged the work as annotated PDFs for executive review and brought feedback into the next iteration.

Managed asset handoff and design QA. I coordinated the delivery of desktop and mobile component designs, imagery treatments, and product illustrations in batches aligned with the build. I organized a shared asset library and coordinated page-by-page design QA before launch.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices — Design sprint planning, daily stand-ups, weekly delivery reviews, reprioritization, executive approvals, and design QA.

Artifacts — Design roadmap and milestone plan, RACI matrix, Asana task board, Excel asset tracker for owners and versions, Figma design system and guidelines, annotated PDF review packs, and a SharePoint asset library.

Outcomes & Impact

The design workstream met its delivery milestones. Content delays shifted the overall launch schedule. The first two regional sites launched, followed by three more, using the same design system, component patterns, and asset library. Regional teams could reuse those materials while adapting content for their markets.

Conference Delivery · 2024–2025

North American User Conferences

Project at a Glance

I managed delivery and budget control for several workstreams across two North American user conferences for a global technology company. My scope included the main stage, audiovisual production for the keynote sessions and digital program, event branding and environmental design, sales enablement materials, attendee engagement, social activities, and merchandise.

Challenge

Registration had its own launch deadline while planning and production for the live event were still underway. Both editions had fixed event dates, and production depended on executive approvals, vendor lead times, venue conditions, and union requirements. As conference work ramped up, I negotiated changes to other regional deadlines and communicated the options and expected gaps in service.

Delivery Approach

Defined ownership and milestones. I developed a RACI matrix with the VP of Marketing and built a shared roadmap in Asana with a three-person planning group. I led workstream kickoffs and briefed regional VPs on schedule changes and proposed alternatives.

Sequenced approvals and production. I facilitated theme workshops, supported executive approvals, and led development of the event’s visual direction and brand system. I brought in external design support and coordinated assets for the website and registration launch while production continued on keynote decks, stage motion graphics, venue signage and wayfinding, and merchandise. I used a production tracker to sequence the work around approvals, vendor lead times, and installation dates.

Prepared teams for the event. I created training guides covering the event website, registration process, event app, and venue wayfinding. I led virtual training before the event and followed up with in-person training at the venue.

Managed on-site delivery and closeout. I used site visits to check designs against venue conditions and documented union requirements for the team. I approved production proofs, checked installations, and coordinated AV handoffs and live keynote production. After the event, I oversaw asset removal, reconciled invoices, and led a retrospective.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices — Stakeholder kickoffs, milestone planning, vendor benchmarking, weekly coordination meetings, daily stand-ups during peak production, weekly and monthly status reporting, virtual and on-site training, and post-event retrospectives.

Artifacts — RACI matrix, Asana roadmap, Excel production tracker covering specifications, quantities, costs, owners, deadlines, and dependencies, venue and union requirements brief, event training guides, status reports, and invoice records.

Outcomes & Impact

I delivered my assigned workstreams within their approved budgets for both editions. The post-event retrospectives documented what worked and identified improvements to planning and production for future conferences.

Post-Merger Integration · 2019

Brand & Operations Integration

Project at a Glance

I led the brand strategy and rollout workstream for a merger that brought four companies together under one identity. I also represented Marketing in the wider merger transition, auditing regional services and operations and reporting milestones, dependencies, and blockers to the Operations Office.

Challenge

The new name and identity had to be ready for the merger announcement while regional teams continued to deliver marketing services. The transition also had to account for differences in how those teams worked, including their workflows, tools, and licence renewal cycles. My audit identified approximately 3,500 physical and digital assets for review.

Delivery Approach

Aligned leadership on the brand direction. I facilitated workshops with the CEO, CMO, and Chief Strategy Officer, then ran regional focus groups and one-on-one interviews to understand market opportunities and stakeholder concerns. I summarized the findings and used them to guide workshops on brand strategy and visual identity.

Audited services, workflows, and assets. I reviewed how regional teams delivered marketing services, identified overlapping tools and opportunities to consolidate licences, and updated service playbooks and workflows for the combined function. I worked with regional leaders to decide which assets to rebrand, consolidate into reusable templates, or retire.

Sequenced the rollout around merger milestones. I grouped deliverables into announcement materials, assets essential to business continuity, and the wider rollout. I aligned the work with the merger’s 30-, 60-, 90-, and 120-day milestones, assigned a dedicated team to the announcement, and organized the remaining work into production sprints.

Coordinated production and handover. During the four-month production period, I ran daily stand-ups and reported weekly to the Operations Office. At closeout, I documented brand guidelines, created a template library and training materials, and facilitated a retrospective.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices — Executive workshops, stakeholder interviews, service and asset audits, scope prioritization, milestone planning, daily stand-ups, weekly transition reporting, and a retrospective.

Artifacts — Brand strategy brief, Smartsheet transition tracker, Excel asset inventory and production tracker, SharePoint audit repository, updated service playbooks and workflows, brand guidelines, template library, and training materials.

Outcomes & Impact

The brand workstream met its scheduled milestones. The new identity rolled out across websites, customer community and support platforms, sales and training materials, event assets, and internal systems and documents, including HR, legal, and finance materials. The updated playbooks and workflows gave the combined marketing function a shared reference for delivering services, and the brand guidelines and template library helped teams apply the new identity after rollout.

Acquisition Communications & Integration · 2019–2025

M&A Announcements & Integration Kickoff

Project at a Glance

I supported confidential announcement planning and early integration across more than 15 acquisitions of varying scale. I managed the announcement production workstream, coordinated schedules and materials with counterparts at acquired companies, and supported the onboarding of incoming teams after each announcement.

Challenge

Each announcement had a fixed release time, and advance access was limited to designated stakeholders. Both organizations needed to align their schedules and prepare communications, design assets, and platform updates. In the final 72 hours, edits had to reach the right teams quickly so they could update materials already staged for release.

Delivery Approach

Planned the announcement workstream. I identified who needed to be involved before the announcement and coordinated production of PR, social media, website, and design assets. I managed filming with an external video agency and staged updates in publishing platforms.

Coordinated readiness across both organizations. I served as the point of contact for counterparts at acquired companies, aligned schedules and milestones, shared brand direction, and surfaced concerns that needed early attention. I tracked asset readiness in Smartsheet, reported progress to the global officer, and coordinated revisions through the final 72- and 24-hour checkpoints.

Supported the handoff into integration. After publication, I continued to track actions in Smartsheet and joined weekly integration meetings. I helped incoming teams gain access to tools, trained them on playbooks and guidelines, and helped plan follow-up actions.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices · Confidential stakeholder coordination, weekly transition meetings for mid- and high-impact acquisitions, cross-company milestone planning, release readiness checks, and onboarding.

Artifacts · Smartsheet transition and asset tracker, coordinated announcement schedules, progress updates, announcement materials, and onboarding playbooks and guidelines.

Outcomes & Impact

I coordinated announcement materials from confidential preparation through publication across more than 15 acquisitions. The shared tracker gave both organizations a way to follow milestones and flag concerns before release, and it carried follow-up actions into the first weeks of integration.

Workplace Project Delivery · October 2025–February 2026

Workplace Branding & Multi-Site Rollout

Project at a Glance

I led the planning and delivery of a workplace rebranding rollout across eight offices. The principal scopes were in Toronto, Richmond in London, Skopje, Lehi, and Melbourne, with smaller interventions in Riyadh, Carmel, and Charleston. I controlled the rollout budget, managed local vendors, and coordinated work from technical handoff through fabrication, installation, and quality review.

Challenge

Each office had different layouts, existing finishes, and documentation. Material availability and vendor production lead times varied by region. I needed to translate a shared brand direction into specifications local vendors could price, fabricate, and install, then build a schedule around those differences.

Delivery Approach

Built the rollout roadmap and tracked dependencies. I collected existing plans, arranged local measurements where records were incomplete, and set milestones around regional fabrication lead times and installation dates. I maintained a master Excel tracker with item-by-item specifications, quantities, costs, progress, and dependencies for each office.

Managed vendors, production, and costs. I handed off marked-up plans, finish specifications, and fabrication details for three-dimensional signage and environmental graphics. I supervised production, reviewed proofs, tracked costs against the approved budget, and reconciled invoices and coordinated payments.

Verified installation and reported progress. I reviewed completed work in person or remotely against the approved drawings and specifications. I prepared weekly executive reports on progress by office, outstanding dependencies, and issues affecting delivery.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices · Milestone and dependency planning, vendor coordination, budget control, proof approvals, invoice review, on-site and remote quality checks, and weekly executive reporting.

Artifacts · Rollout roadmap, master Excel tracker, existing-condition plans and markups, regional finish specifications, signage fabrication details, vendor proofs, and weekly status reports.

Outcomes & Impact

All priority offices were completed by February 2026. The rollout applied a consistent visual identity across locations while adapting finishes, signage, and graphics to each site’s layout, scope, and locally available materials.

Residential Project Coordination · Mexico City · April–July 2026

Residential Rehabilitation

Project at a Glance

I coordinated repairs to the water-storage system, drainage, and exterior of a 1960s residence in Mexico City. As freelance project coordinator, I managed scope, contractor quotations, procurement, scheduling, budget tracking, and quality reviews. I coordinated delivery from Toronto following a preliminary site visit.

Challenge

Anticipated water-supply shortages made restoring the existing underground tank and pump a priority. The drainage work required confirming how the property connected to the municipal sewer, and the original drawings needed to be updated to reflect changes to the house. I had to sequence excavation, demolition, repairs, and reinstatement across multiple trades.

Delivery Approach

Verified conditions and defined the scope. I walked through the property with the client, reconciled the original drawings with site measurements, and produced an updated AutoCAD drawing set. I documented the drainage route and worked with contractors to define the repair scopes.

Compared contractors and built the schedule. I compared quotations, costs, and proposed timelines by trade, then consolidated the shortlisted options into a budget for client approval. I built a project plan that sequenced the underground work, reinstatement, and façade repairs.

Monitored delivery and kept the client informed. I tracked contractor progress and spending remotely, reviewed work quality, and coordinated testing before the water-storage system returned to service. Weekly reports used photos and plain-language notes to show completed work, spending against budget, and any new findings or upcoming decisions.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices · Client scope review, contractor comparison, procurement coordination, schedule and budget tracking, remote quality reviews, and weekly client reporting.

Artifacts · Existing-condition AutoCAD drawings, contractor quotations, itemized budget, project schedule, and photographic progress reports.

Outcomes & Impact

The water-storage system returned to service, the drainage connection was repaired, and the façade was repaired and repainted. The work finished on schedule and within the approved budget. The client also received an updated drawing set documenting the residence’s existing layout.

Office Fit-Out & Mezzanine Addition · Mexico City · 2013

Workplace Design & Construction Delivery

Project at a Glance

I developed the design with the principal architect and served as construction coordinator for an office fit-out and mezzanine addition. Over approximately six to nine months, I coordinated specialist drawings, permit submissions, construction scheduling, budget tracking, and client reporting through handover.

Challenge

The client needed a workplace that could support daily office work, training, and group gatherings within a single commercial unit. Adding a mezzanine meant coordinating the architectural design with structural, electrical, plumbing, and safety requirements. Consultant drawings, permit submissions, and client approvals for materials and prototypes had to align with the construction schedule.

Delivery Approach

Coordinated consultant deliverables and permits. I aligned specialist drawing schedules with the architectural programme, incorporated consultant inputs into the technical package, and coordinated the drawings and documentation needed for permit and licence submissions.

Tracked schedule, costs, and approvals. I maintained the construction schedule in Microsoft Project and used Excel to track drawings and other deliverables by owner, status, and due date. I gathered material samples and prototypes for client approval, tracked spending against budget, processed invoices, and handled biweekly payments for the construction team.

Reviewed construction through handover. I visited the site weekly to check work against the approved drawings, document progress, and revise plans or details as site conditions required. I reported progress, schedule, and spending to the client each week, carried out final quality reviews, and led the formal handover and walkthrough of how to use the space.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices · Consultant coordination, dependency tracking, permit coordination, material and prototype approvals, cost monitoring, weekly site reviews and client reporting, and formal handover.

Artifacts · Microsoft Project Gantt schedule, Excel drawing register and permit tracker, coordinated technical drawing package, budget and payment records, and weekly construction progress reports.

Outcomes & Impact

The completed fit-out and mezzanine gave the client dedicated work areas and a reconfigurable meeting space for training, presentations, and group sessions. I coordinated the transition from construction to occupancy through final quality reviews, documentation, and a client walkthrough.

Technology Procurement · Enterprise AI Readiness Program

AI Tool Evaluation & Procurement

Project at a Glance

I partnered with the global procurement lead to evaluate enterprise AI tools for marketing content creation and management. I gathered requirements from regional leaders, compared three external providers and an internal Azure OpenAI option, and prepared a recommendation for executive review.

Challenge

Regional teams had different content workflows, and some existing tools already offered AI capabilities. The procurement decision depended on understanding where a new tool would add value, how much it would cost to implement, and how each option aligned with company procurement and compliance requirements.

Delivery Approach

Defined requirements from regional use cases. I audited existing tools and capabilities as part of the broader AI readiness program. I gathered examples from regional leaders and documented the current workflow, available alternatives, and expected benefits for each use case.

Compared external providers and an internal option. Working with the global procurement lead, I assessed each option against the same use cases. I compared functional fit, estimated costs, implementation requirements, benefits, and limitations, and mapped each option against the company’s procurement and compliance policies.

Prepared the executive recommendation. I turned the assessment into an executive briefing and presentation that explained how each option would address the documented needs and what it would take to implement.

Delivery Practices & Artifacts

Practices · Stakeholder requirements gathering, technology audit, vendor evaluation, internal versus external options analysis, cost and implementation assessment, and policy alignment review.

Artifacts · Tool inventory, regional use-case documentation, options comparison, executive briefing and presentation, and procurement recommendation.

Outcomes & Impact

I completed the evaluation and delivered a recommendation that gave leadership a basis for deciding whether to procure an external tool, develop an internal solution, or make greater use of existing tools. My involvement concluded at the recommendation stage.
